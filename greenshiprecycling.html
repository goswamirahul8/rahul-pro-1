Sustainability Infrastructure

Environmental Protection Standards

Our yard is engineered to eliminate environmental degradation during vessel recycling through advanced containment and lifting capabilities.

01

Zero Intertidal Spillage

Taking scientific measures to avoid metal slag, hazardous residue, or oil touching the intertidal zone during all cutting operations.

02

Impermeable Concrete Floor

100% concrete paved yard and 30-metre Jetty. Vessels land safely 5 metres from the Jetty on impervious ground.

03

Metal Slag Collector

100% collection of metal slag during side-shell cutting, preventing any environmental entry into the sea bed.

04

Environmental Impact Assessment

Quarterly scientific testing of Intertidal Water, RO Water, Soil, Noise, and Ambient Air quality by certified labs.

05

100% Overhead Heavy Lifting

Zero contact with the intertidal zone achieved using 150-Ton and 250-Ton capacity heavy-duty crawler cranes.

Zero-Harm Philosophy

Occupational Health & Safety (HSE)

Ship recycling is labor-intensive. We scientifically mitigate operational hazards through strict preventive safeguards.

Tested & Certified Equipment

All equipment undergoes regular testing by Government-approved 3rd party certification vendors.

Flashback Arrestors

Installed on all gas cylinders and torch sides to eliminate fire and backfire hazards completely.

3-Way Cutting Torches

Specialized long/short gas cutting torches for easy, ergonomic, and secure vessel cutting.

Asbestos Disposal (Best Practice)

Certified Hazmat experts collaborate with approved vendors for safe removal and containment of asbestos.

NEBOSH PPE Standards

3M 6300 masks with 2091 P100 filters, fire-retardant boiler suits, safety helmets, and cut-resistant gloves.

Continuous HSE Training

Regular internal safety workshops and skill upgrades for all employees and yard workers.
